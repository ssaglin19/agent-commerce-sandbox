# Agent-commerce market notes

Dated notes on public-source reporting about agent payments, commerce, and trust. These entries summarize claims in the linked sources; secondary-source reporting is labeled.

## 2026-10-02

- **Agentic payment proof points and Verifiable Intent (secondary report):** The Agent Report describes controlled live agent-purchase proof points involving Worldline, ING, Mastercard and Visa in Europe, and notes these are not broad commercial availability. It reports that Verifiable Intent is intended to create tamper-resistant evidence of what a consumer authorized and is aligned with Google's AP2 and UCP. Source: [The Agent Report, "Agentic Commerce Leaves the Sandbox"](https://the-agent-report.com/2026/10/agentic-commerce-production-rails-2026/).

- **Mastercard Agent Pay trust and intelligence services:** Mastercard says it is testing in the US a probability score for whether a transaction was initiated by an AI agent, alongside planned identity, intent, behavioral and fraud signals for issuer and merchant authorization decisions. Mastercard names Cloudflare and Skyfire as collaborators. Source: [The Paypers, "Mastercard adds AI agent trust services to Agent Pay"](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/mastercard-expands-agent-pay-with-trust-and-intelligence-services).

- **Meow on business agent workflows:** Meow describes using its existing scoped spend controls, multi-entity accounts, corporate cards and payment features in business payment workflows involving agents. Source: [Meow, "Building Agentic Payment Workflows"](https://www.meow.com/blog/building-agentic-payment-workflows-meow-s-scoped-controls-make-it-safe-to-ship).

## 2026-10-03

- **EMVCo agentic payments framework consultation:** EMVCo closed public comments Sept. 30 on its proposed card-payment framework. Its proposed Intent Services would support registering, referencing, retrieving and managing consumer-authorized intent before, during and after transactions. EMVCo may also consider KYA capabilities and agentic transaction indicators; its representative said historical intent may be needed for dispute resolution. Feedback review is underway and future specifications are not yet decided. This is a standards proposal, not a launched feature. Source: [PYMNTS, "EMVCo Framework Targets Consumer Intent in Agentic Payments"](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/emvco-framework-targets-consumer-intent-agentic-payments/).

## 2026-10-04

- **CellCog Spend consumer purchasing launch:** CellCog says its Spend feature is live for AI employees. Users connect a Link wallet and approve each exact merchant and amount in Link; an approved purchase uses a one-time card. CellCog says multiple employees in one organization can share the wallet, with employee-specific purchase switches, optional caps, purchase histories and a Spend app view of purchases and status. This is a company-authored product description; it describes aggregation across CellCog's own employees, not independent third-party agents. Source: [CellCog, "Your AI Employees Can Now Buy Things, With Your OK"](https://cellcog.ai/blog/ai-employee-spend/).

## 2026-10-05

- **Snaplii Cash for AI-agent purchases:** Snaplii says users can fund a separate Snaplii Cash wallet, set a spending limit, and use the balance for purchases by agents without exposing their card or bank details. Snaplii says it works with Meta Muse today and is built for other consumer agents, including Instinct; purchases can earn cashback. It also describes an MCP-compatible Agent-to-Merchant interface with one-time prefunded credentials. These are company claims; the announcement does not establish consolidated transaction histories or dispute tools across independent agents. Source: [ACCESS Newswire, "Snaplii Offers a Separate Wallet for AI Agent Spending, With Cashback"](https://week.marketminute.com/article/accwirecq-2026-10-5-as-muse-tops-the-app-store-and-instinct-raises-1-billion-snaplii-offers-a-separate-wallet-for-ai-agent-spending-with-cashback).

## 2026-10-06

- **Wallets, approval and activity controls:** Worldpay describes its Agentic Commerce Platform as supporting agent authentication, routing, payment processing and transaction monitoring for merchants. Its post explains a planned Agent Wallet with per-transaction authorizations, customer approval notifications and detailed payment records. These are provider/merchant-side capabilities, not evidence of a consumer cross-provider ledger. Source: [Worldpay, "Agentic Commerce, A New Era of Payments"](https://www.worldpay.com/us/blog/agentic-commerce-a-new-era-of-payments).

## 2026-10-07

- **Google's agentic payments standardization and access controls:** Google's Agent Payments Protocol documentation describes Cart Mandates and payment-method authorization, including a user approval step for the specific transaction. The protocol is intended to communicate transaction context among agent, merchant and payment provider; this is standardization, not a consumer dashboard or deployed account-wide control product. Source: [Google, AP2 documentation](https://ap2-protocol.org/).

## 2026-10-08

- **Visa Trusted Agent Protocol:** Visa says TAP provides a way to identify authorized AI agents and verify their interactions with merchants, using cryptographic signatures. Visa positions it for commerce and bot authentication; its public overview does not establish a user-facing spending dashboard or provider-independent receipts. Source: [Visa Intelligent Commerce](https://www.visa.com/en-us/solutions/intelligent-commerce).

## 2026-10-09

- **Skyfire agent payment infrastructure:** Skyfire describes identity-linked agent wallets and payment capabilities for AI services. The materials focus on provider infrastructure and machine payments; a consumer multi-agent spending view is not described in the cited source. Source: [Skyfire](https://skyfire.xyz/).

This supports card-level implementation, not a verified consumer-facing independent-agent dashboard. Source: [Lithic, agentic commerce](https://www.lithic.com/blog/agentic-commerce).

## 2026-10-19

- **Vivi Money announces an AI shopping and payment experience with in-app approval:** Australian FinTech reports Vivi Shop & Pay can compare products across retailers, use Vivi's identity-verified account and customer-connected Open Banking accounts, and complete shopping or travel checkout only after purchase details are reviewed and approved in the Vivi app. The report says the agent receives neither card details nor wallet access; after approval, Vivi generates a single-use Visa credential restricted to the merchant and amount. It reports two completed Visa-network demos and says Shop & Pay is expected for Premium Metal customers from November, with a waitlist open; the announcement does not establish broad availability. The report also says customers may connect other preferred assistants, including ChatGPT or Claude, to Vivi for payments, while approval remains in the Vivi app. This is a close consumer-side validation and potential competitor/channel: it joins an existing personal-finance account, connected-account context, agent shopping and purchase approval in one surface. Its stated approval/payment surface is Vivi-routed rather than an independent cross-wallet/cross-processor ledger, and broad rollout remains pending. Sources: [Australian FinTech, "Vivi Money launches Vivi Shop & Pay, letting AI shop for you without the keys to your wallet"](https://australianfintech.com.au/vivi-money-launches-vivi-shop-pay-letting-ai-shop-for-you-without-the-keys-to-your-wallet/); [Vivi](https://www.vivi.com.au/) (existing finance/connected-account product overview; Shop & Pay not listed there).

- **Proof and Circle announce an x401/x402 agent-identity/payment demo:** Proof's Oct. 19 release says the companies will demonstrate at Money20/20 an agent presenting a verified Proof credential and signed approval for a specific purchase via x401, then paying in USDC over x402 from a Circle Agent Wallet. The release says the merchant receives signed evidence of who approved the purchase for possible dispute use. This is a conference demo integrating two protocols and Circle's wallet, not general production availability or proof of a dispute outcome. Source: [Proof, "Proof and Circle Collaborate at Money20/20 to Demonstrate Agentic Commerce With x401, x402"](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/19/3165510/0/en/proof-and-circle-collaborate-at-money20-20-to-demonstrate-agentic-commerce-with-x401-x402.html).

- **Stripe Link activity growth (secondary report):** The Decoder reports Stripe's assertion that Link agent-wallet transaction count grew 38x and volume 20x over a recent period, without disclosing the small starting base or proving market-scale adoption. This is a secondary report and doesn't change the Link-specific product scope described above. Source: [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/stripe-says-transactions-through-its-ai-agent-wallet-have-grown-38x/).

- **TomoIQ Money Finder:** TomoIQ describes personal-finance guidance and discovery, not agent purchase execution or spend-control tooling. Source: [TomoIQ](https://tomoiq.com/).

## 2026-10-20

- **OnePay Agentic Banking (close consumer threat/validation):** OnePay says consumer agents can use a separately funded pocket with a virtual card; connected agents share the pool, with per-transaction and monthly limits, notifications, single-use credentials and revocation. Rollout is staged and initially limited to Builder Card customers. This is meaningful consumer-side validation for controlled multi-agent spending, but it is confined to OnePay's own pocket and does not establish cross-provider aggregation or dispute-grade receipts. Source: [OnePay, Agentic Banking](https://www.onepay.com/newsroom/onepay-agenticbanking).

- **Mastercard Agent Pay for Machines developer Early Access:** Mastercard says buyer/seller APIs, a toolkit, sandbox and documentation are open to developers after a business demonstration using nine x402 payments across two service providers under one approved task budget. This is a protocol/infrastructure milestone, not consumer shopping or general commercial availability. Source: [Mastercard, Agent Pay for Machines in action](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/press/2026/october/Mastercard-Agent-Pay-for-Machines-in-action.html).

- **Visa, BILL and Billtrust live B2B invoice-payment pilot:** The companies say participating buyers' already-approved invoice instructions can be executed end-to-end by authenticated agents through supplier portals, with tokenized credentials and visibility into authorization and completion. This validates approval-bound business payment execution, not a consumer product. Source: [Visa, BILL and Billtrust](https://investor.visa.com/news/news-details/2026/Visa-BILL-and-Billtrust-Launch-First-Live-Pilot-for-Trusted-AI-Agent-Payments-Between-Buyer-and-Supplier-Platforms/default.aspx).

- **Circle managed payment acceptance and reporting:** Circle says CPN Managed Payments supports eligible agent-facing businesses with payment screening, attribution, reconciliation, reporting and payouts. This is seller-side infrastructure; it may improve merchant records, but the source does not establish consumer dashboards, independent cross-agent history or dispute outcomes. Source: [Circle, Managed Agentic Payments](https://www.circle.com/blog/turn-agent-traffic-into-revenue-with-cpn-managed-payments).

## 2026-10-21

- **Stripe Treasury for agents:** Stripe announced agent access to business Treasury information and financial operations through MCP, including balance checks, invoice payments, transfers between accounts and currency conversion. Current Treasury agent docs mark balance-summary, currency-conversion, send-money, recipient-setup and transfer tools as public preview. Stripe says live money movement requires review by default. Its announcement describes creating cards and setting agent spending limits as capabilities coming in the next few weeks, so these should not be treated as generally available now. This is a business-finance/dashboard integration, not a consumer cross-agent or cross-provider spending product. Sources: [Stripe, "Introducing Stripe Treasury for agents"](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/treasury-for-agents); [Stripe Treasury for agents docs](https://docs.stripe.com/treasury/agents).

- **AgentPay product claims (availability not established):** AgentPay's site advertises per-agent budgets, purchase approvals, single-use virtual Visa cards, a dashboard, transaction/activity records and hierarchical agent budgets. However, the page labels the US cards beta/"going live soon" and shows waitlist/coming-soon pricing. Its status page says the API and site are operational, which does not establish public customer access or general availability. No dated launch announcement was verified; treat this as a surfaced product signal, not a confirmed Oct. 21 launch. Sources: [AgentPay](https://www.agentpay.la/); [AgentPay status](https://status.agentpay.la/).

- **BankRails product claims (developer access by waitlist):** BankRails markets business ACH/wire agent payments with per-agent spending limits, approval queues, agent identity, transaction dashboards and ledgers. Its site says developer access is first and invites users to join a waitlist. No dated launch or operational customer evidence was verified; this is business-payment infrastructure and not evidence of a consumer product launch. Source: [BankRails](https://bankrails.ai/).

- **YourAgentPays product claims (launch status unclear):** YourAgentPays markets non-custodial x402 agent wallets, category spending rules, notifications, revocation and a transaction dashboard. The first-party page has no dated launch or verifiable operational status. Treat it as a product-positioning signal, not a confirmed launch or proof of broad availability. Source: [YourAgentPays](https://youragentpays.com/).

- **Visa's Oct. 21 business-payments release:** The release highlights the Visa/BILL/Billtrust B2B invoice-payment pilot reported Oct. 20, rather than establishing a new consumer-facing agent spend product. Source: [Visa, "Enhanced capabilities for business payments"](https://investor.visa.com/news/news-details/2026/Visa-Announces-Enhanced-Capabilities-That-Can-Bring-Trust-and-Control-to-the-Future-of-Business-Payments/default.aspx).
